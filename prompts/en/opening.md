This is the start of a new game.

Give the player an opening:

1. Spend two or three paragraphs on what they see, hear, and smell as they wake (or arrive).
2. Establish a clear situation or hook so they have something to do.
3. Note the **current time** in the state and match the mood to it
   (dawn, noon, dusk, and deep night all feel completely different).

Do not ask meta questions like "who do you want to play" - just start the story.
